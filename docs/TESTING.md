Three independent review rounds covered core/data, native UI/UX and packaged export. Uncooperative same-user writers still have a rename gap because POSIX does not offer an atomic inode-conditional rename. Power-loss durability remains unverified. A multi-action rule is not an atomic transaction, and stability observations cannot prove a writer is finished. Inspect uncertain history and recorded paths manually; keep backups and start with disposable folders.
